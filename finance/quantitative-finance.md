# Quantitative Finance

Courses on mathematical and computational methods in finance — stochastic calculus, derivatives pricing, risk management, and statistical inference.

> **Access notes**
> - MIT OCW: entirely free, no registration required (archived materials)

---

## MIT

### [Topics in Mathematics with Applications in Finance (18.S096)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s096-topics-in-mathematics-with-applications-in-finance-fall-2013/)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Undergraduate / Graduate
- **Instructors:** Peter Kempthorne, Choongbum Lee, Vasily Strela, Jake Xia
- **Description:** Mathematical concepts used in the financial industry — taught by MIT faculty and industry practitioners; bridges applied mathematics and probability/statistics with real-world finance applications.

### [Analytics of Finance (15.450)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-450-analytics-of-finance-fall-2010/)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Instructor:** Leonid Kogan
- **Description:** Quantitative methods for finance — financial econometrics, dynamic optimization, Monte Carlo simulation, and Itô stochastic calculus; applied to portfolio management, risk management, derivatives, and trading strategies.
