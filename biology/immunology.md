# Immunology

Courses on cellular and molecular immunity, immune-related disease, and immunotherapy.

> **Access notes**
> - MIT OCW: entirely free, no registration required (archived materials)
> - No verified standalone immunology MOOC was found for Stanford or CMU on Coursera/edX. Stanford's *Stories of Infection* (see [virology.md](./virology.md)) integrates immunology content.
> - Harvard HMX offers paid immunology courses (~$495–$1,025) not listed here.

---

## MIT

### Cellular and Molecular Immunology (HST.176)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-176-cellular-and-molecular-immunology-fall-2005/
- **Instructor:** Shiv Pillai (Harvard Medical School / MIT HST joint faculty)
- **Description:** Comprehensive immunology covering lymphocyte development, antigen receptors, MHC structure, cytokines, leukocyte-endothelial interactions, and immunologically mediated diseases.

### A Double-Edged Sword: Cellular Immunity in Health and Disease (7.342)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate (advanced seminar)
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-342-a-double-edged-sword-cellular-immunity-in-health-and-disease-fall-2018/
- **Instructor:** Haiting Ma
- **Description:** Cellular immunity in depth — cell death, immune cell signaling, and clinical applications including immune checkpoint blockade in cancer.
