# Virology

Courses on virus biology, host-pathogen interactions, emerging infectious diseases, and epidemiology.

> **Access notes**
> - MIT OCW: entirely free, no registration required (archived materials)
> - Stanford / Coursera: free to audit
> - No verified standalone virology MOOC was found for Harvard or CMU on Coursera/edX.

---

## MIT

### COVID-19, SARS-CoV-2, and the Pandemic (7.00)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Undergraduate
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-00-covid-19-sars-cov-2-and-the-pandemic-fall-2021/
- **Instructors:** Richard Young, Facundo Batista; guest lecturers include Anthony Fauci, Rochelle Walensky, Dan Barouch, Arup Chakraborty
- **Description:** Full lecture course on coronavirus biology, host cell interactions, immunology, epidemiology, clinical disease, vaccines, and therapeutics.

### Virus-Host Interactions in Infectious Diseases (7.346)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate (advanced seminar)
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-346-virus-host-interactions-in-infectious-diseases-spring-2013/
- **Description:** How viruses (HIV, influenza, dengue) hijack host cell machinery — covers protein-protein interactions, intracellular trafficking, and antiviral protein induction.

### Unusual Biology: The Science of Emerging Pathogens (7.340)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate (seminar)
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-340-unusual-biology-the-science-of-emerging-pathogens-spring-2013/
- **Description:** Primary literature-based seminar on molecular and cellular mechanisms of host-pathogen interactions in emerging infectious diseases.

### Microbes at War: Mechanisms That Drive Infectious Diseases (7.341)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate (seminar)
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-341-microbes-at-war-the-mechanisms-that-drive-infectious-diseases-fall-2022/
- **Description:** Molecular interactions between pathogens and hosts that enable infectious diseases, explored through primary research literature.

## Stanford

### Stories of Infection
- **Platform:** Coursera / Stanford Online
- **Level:** Introductory
- **Links:** https://www.coursera.org/learn/stories-of-infection · https://online.stanford.edu/courses/som-y0008-stories-infection
- **Instructor:** Charles Prober, MD
- **Description:** Patient-centered course following individuals through a range of infectious diseases, integrating microbiology, pathophysiology, and immunology. Free to audit.

### Epidemiology of Infectious Diseases (EPI 231)
- **Platform:** Stanford Online
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://online.stanford.edu/courses/epi231-epidemiology-infectious-diseases
- **Description:** Role of viral and bacterial agents in disease, epidemiological modeling, and molecular techniques used in disease investigation.
