# Genomics & Bioinformatics

Courses covering computational biology, genome sequencing, gene regulation, and bioinformatics tooling.

> **Access notes**
> - MIT OCW: entirely free, no registration required (archived materials)
> - HarvardX on edX: free to audit, certificate requires payment
> - Stanford Online: varies — many free to audit via Coursera

---

## MIT

### Foundations of Computational and Systems Biology (7.91J)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Undergraduate / Graduate
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-91j-foundations-of-computational-and-systems-biology-spring-2014/
- **Instructors:** Christopher Burge, David Gifford, Ernest Fraenkel
- **Description:** Sequence alignment, motif finding, genome assembly, structural modeling, and network modeling. Full lecture videos available on YouTube.

### Computational Biology: Genomes, Networks, Evolution (6.047 / 6.878)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-047-computational-biology-fall-2015/
- **Instructor:** Manolis Kellis
- **Description:** Algorithmic and machine learning foundations of computational biology — from genome sequencing and gene regulation to evolutionary genomics and network analysis.

### Genomics and Computational Biology (HST.508)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-508-genomics-and-computational-biology-fall-2002/
- **Description:** Sequence-structure-function relationships in biological networks, with applications to medicine, drug discovery, and genetic engineering.

### Computational Functional Genomics (7.90J)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-90j-computational-functional-genomics-spring-2005/
- **Description:** Systems biology and functional genomics — genome structure, transcriptional regulation, and microarray technologies.

## Stanford

### Translational Bioinformatics (BIOMEDIN 217)
- **Platform:** Stanford Online
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://online.stanford.edu/courses/biomedin217-translational-bioinformatics
- **Instructor:** Atul Butte
- **Description:** Convergence of molecular bioinformatics, biostatistics, and statistical genetics for genomic medicine.

### Genomics and the Other Omics: Comprehensive Essentials (XGEN 102)
- **Platform:** Stanford Online
- **Level:** Introductory / Intermediate
- **Link:** https://online.stanford.edu/courses/xgen102-genomics-and-other-omics-comprehensive-essentials
- **Description:** Genomic technologies and computational approaches across genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics, with applications in diagnostics and treatment.

### Representations and Algorithms for Computational Molecular Biology (BMDS 214)
- **Platform:** Stanford Online
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Link:** https://online.stanford.edu/courses/bmds214-representations-and-algorithms-computational-molecular-biology
- **Description:** Computing with DNA, RNA, proteins, and small molecules — sequence analysis, 3D structure analysis, and high-throughput functional data.

## Harvard

### Data Analysis for Genomics Professional Certificate (PH525x)
- **Platform:** edX (HarvardX)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Link:** https://www.edx.org/certificates/professional-certificate/harvardx-data-analysis-for-genomics
- **Instructors:** Rafael Irizarry, Michael Love, Vincent Carey
- **Description:** Multi-course series using R/Bioconductor — covers statistical inference, RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, DNA methylation, and cloud-scale genomic data.

### Introduction to Bioconductor
- **Platform:** edX (HarvardX)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Link:** https://www.edx.org/learn/data-science/harvard-university-introduction-to-bioconductor
- **Instructors:** Rafael Irizarry, Vincent Carey, Michael Love
- **Description:** Open-source R/Bioconductor framework for genomic data analysis, including parallel computing and reproducible research workflows.

### Case Studies in Functional Genomics
- **Platform:** edX (HarvardX)
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Link:** https://www.edx.org/learn/data-analysis/harvard-university-case-studies-in-functional-genomics
- **Instructors:** Vincent Carey, Michael Love, Rafael Irizarry
- **Description:** Applies bioinformatics methods to real-world RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, and DNA methylation datasets using R/Bioconductor.

## CMU

### Bioinformatics Specialization
- **Platform:** Coursera
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Link:** https://www.coursera.org/specializations/bioinformatics
- **Instructors:** Phillip Compeau (CMU), Pavel Pevzner (UCSD)
- **Description:** Seven-course series covering genome sequencing, sequence alignment, molecular evolution, and mutation detection. Based on the textbook *Bioinformatics Algorithms*. Formally listed under UCSD on Coursera; Compeau is CMU faculty and co-created the content.

### Biology Meets Programming: Bioinformatics for Beginners
- **Platform:** Coursera
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Link:** https://www.coursera.org/learn/bioinformatics
- **Instructors:** Phillip Compeau (CMU), Pavel Pevzner (UCSD)
- **Description:** Entry-level course teaching Python through bioinformatics problems such as DNA pattern finding and genome replication. Part of the Bioinformatics Specialization above.
