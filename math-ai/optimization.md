## MIT

### Network Optimization (15.082J / 6.855J / ESD.78J)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Fall 2010
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-082j-network-optimization-fall-2010/
- **Instructor:** Prof. James Orlin
- **Description:** Theory and practice of network flows and its extensions, treating network flow problems as a specialized category of linear programming. Covers shortest path, maximum flow, minimum cost flow, and multi-commodity flow. Applications span transportation, logistics, manufacturing, project management, and finance. Includes lecture notes, problem sets, exams, and animations.
