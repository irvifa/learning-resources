# Computer Vision

Courses on machine vision, pattern recognition, and visual perception from MIT's Brain and Cognitive Sciences department.

> **Access notes**
> - MIT OCW: entirely free, no registration required

---

## MIT

### Pattern Recognition for Machine Vision (9.913)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Fall 2004
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/9-913-pattern-recognition-for-machine-vision-fall-2004/
- **Instructors:** Dr. Bernd Heisele, Dr. Yuri Ivanov
- **Department:** Brain and Cognitive Sciences
- **Description:** Applies pattern recognition methods to machine vision. Covers image formation and processing, feature extraction, biological object recognition, Bayesian decision theory, and clustering. Bridges cognitive science and AI through signal processing and classification techniques. Includes lecture notes, assignments, and simulation videos.

### Special Topics in Vision Science (9.357)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Fall 2001
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/9-357-special-topics-in-vision-science-fall-2001/
- **Instructor:** Prof. Edward Adelson
- **Department:** Brain and Cognitive Sciences
- **Description:** Advanced seminar on current research in human and machine vision, with Fall 2001 focused on the perception of materials. Draws from optics, psychophysics, computer graphics, and computer vision. Participants discuss contemporary research literature from multiple perspectives.
