# Machine Learning

Courses covering machine learning theory, algorithms, and modern generative AI from MIT.

> **Access notes**
> - MIT OCW: entirely free, no registration required

---

## MIT

### Machine Learning (6.867)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Fall 2006
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-867-machine-learning-fall-2006/
- **Instructors:** Rohit Singh, Prof. Tommi Jaakkola, Ali Mohammad
- **Description:** Introductory survey of machine learning concepts and algorithms. Spans classification, linear regression, boosting, support vector machines, hidden Markov models, and Bayesian networks. Emphasizes statistical inference as the foundation for most methods. Includes lecture notes, problem sets with solutions, exams, and projects.

### Prediction: Machine Learning and Statistics (15.097)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Spring 2012
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-097-prediction-machine-learning-and-statistics-spring-2012/
- **Instructor:** Prof. Cynthia Rudin
- **Description:** Integrates machine learning and classical statistics around the central theme of prediction from data. Covers the most important topics from both fields, focusing on how they converge on generalization and prediction challenges across scientific disciplines. Topics include algorithms, applied mathematics, and probability and statistics.

### Topics in Multiagent Learning (6.S890)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Fall 2024
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-s890-topics-in-multiagent-learning-fall-2024/
- **Instructor:** Prof. Gabriele Farina
- **Description:** Advanced treatment of multi-agent systems where several learning agents pursue competing or cooperative objectives. Integrates game theory, optimization, and learning theory. Topics include matrix games, imperfect information games, equilibrium concepts and computation, and learning algorithm complexity. Real-world applications include AI for Go, poker, Diplomacy, and Stratego.

### Machine Learning for Healthcare (6.S897)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Spring 2019
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-s897-machine-learning-for-healthcare-spring-2019/
- **Instructors:** Prof. Peter Szolovits, Prof. David Sontag
- **Description:** ML applications in clinical settings — risk stratification, disease progression modeling, precision medicine, diagnosis, subtype discovery, and improving clinical workflows. Covers medical imaging and public health applications. Includes lecture videos, notes, and project assignments.

### Foundation Models and Generative AI (6.S087)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Undergraduate
- **Term:** January IAP 2024
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-s087-foundation-models-and-generative-ai-january-iap-2024/
- **Instructor:** Rickard Gabrielsson
- **Description:** Non-technical lecture series exploring breakthrough AI technologies including large language models, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion. Covers supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and self-supervised learning, concluding with the practical and foundational implications of foundation models for science and business. Open to all backgrounds.
