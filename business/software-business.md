## MIT

### The Software Business (15.358)
- **Platform:** MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Term:** Fall 2005
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-358-the-software-business-fall-2005/
- **Instructor:** Prof. Michael Cusumano
- **Description:** Seminar-style course for aspiring software entrepreneurs, managers, and industry professionals. Examines business practices relevant to software companies and organizations heavily dependent on software, including e-business, financial services, and embedded systems. Includes written assignments.
